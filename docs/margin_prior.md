## Starting each team from last season's margin

Generated by `python -m evaluation.select_margin_prior --output docs/margin_prior.md`. Each candidate shrinks a team's season margin toward a share of its previous season's margin instead of toward 0: (n × margin + 10 × prior) / (n + 10). It is trained as the simulation trains at the cutoff and scored on the rest of the season: the error of each team's expected final win total, the Brier score of its playoff odds (10,000 simulated finishes, top 8 per conference), and log loss on the remaining games. Lower is better for all three.

Chosen on November 4 of 2022-23, 2023-24, 2024-25: lowest win-total error; the current model is kept if within 0.1 wins of it, otherwise the lowest playoff Brier among those within it wins, and the winner must not raise the February 1 error by more than 0.1. 2025-26 is reported once.

For reference, giving every team the same 16-in-30 playoff chance scores a playoff Brier of 0.2489.
