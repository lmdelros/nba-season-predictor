## Win-probability model vs. baselines

Generated by `python -m evaluation.evaluate_win_model --output docs/results.md`. Accuracy counts a pick as the side given at least a 50% chance; log loss and Brier score measure probability quality, and lower is better for both.

### Across seasons: train 2021-22 to 2024-25, test 2025-26 (headline)

Every method is trained on all 4 training seasons and scored on every 2025-26 game, none of which it saw.

Train: 4920 games (2021-10-19 to 2025-04-13). Test: 1230 games (2025-10-21 to 2026-04-12).

| Method | Accuracy | Log loss | Brier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home team always wins | 55.4% | 0.687 | 0.247 |
| Better record so far wins | 66.9% | 0.631 | 0.220 |
| Logistic regression, original 13 features | 65.3% | 0.622 | 0.216 |
| Gradient boosting, same features | 65.1% | 0.628 | 0.218 |
| Logistic regression (this project) | 68.6% | 0.608 | 0.209 |
